How this was worked out

Every course title, topic line and outcome was searched for the terms listed under each skill in the Undergraduate Mathematics taxonomy (48 skills). Matching ignores case and punctuation, and the longest phrase wins (“partial differential equations” counts for PDEs, not ODEs). No AI or statistics are involved: each mark in this report traces back to a phrase in a syllabus.

Limits: a syllabus can teach a skill without naming it, and naming it does not show depth. Treat a gap as a question for the committee, not a verdict. Peer findings are only as good as the peer syllabi supplied.
